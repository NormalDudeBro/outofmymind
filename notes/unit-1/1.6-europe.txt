o Feudal politics
-> Europe c. 1200 had fragmented political authority.
-> Feudal ties exchanged land rights for military service.
-> Lords granted fiefs to vassals who pledged loyalty.
-> Nobles held local courts and fielded armed retainers.
-> Kings bargained with nobles rather than ruling uniformly.
-> Decentralization varied; towns also claimed autonomy.
o Monarchies
-> France's Capetians enlarged royal lands and courts.
-> England used royal courts and officials to extend rule.
-> Magna Carta (1215) limited royal power for elites.
-> Parliament gave nobles and towns a voice in taxation.
-> Iberian Christian kingdoms expanded into Muslim lands.
-> Taxes and paid armies helped monarchies centralize.
o Byzantine state
-> Byzantine emperors upheld Roman law and bureaucracy.
-> Orthodox Christianity reinforced imperial legitimacy.
-> Unlike feudal Europe, Byzantium had imperial offices.
-> Crusaders sacked Constantinople in 1204, weakening rule.
-> Restored in 1261, the state faced shrinking territory.

o Religious communities
-> Latin Christianity linked western Europe under the pope.
-> Clergy ran schools; monasteries preserved learned texts.
-> Church courts and landholding bolstered religious power.
-> Judaism endured amid legal restrictions and expulsions.
-> Islam shaped Iberian cities, trade, and scholarship.
-> Crusades mobilized Christians and deepened religious rifts.
o Manorial agriculture
-> Manorialism organized estates around peasant labor.
-> Serfs owed dues and labor; they were bound to the land.
-> Free peasants also farmed; most Europeans lived rurally.
-> Heavy plows and three-field rotation raised some yields.
-> Agricultural surplus supported population and town growth.
-> Lords retained land power despite expanding markets.
o Social and economic change
-> Guilds regulated crafts; merchants gained urban influence.
-> Mediterranean trade linked Europe with Islamic markets.
-> Black Death killed millions and reduced labor supplies.
-> Labor scarcity raised wages and weakened some serfdom.
-> European hierarchies endured despite peasant revolts.
